Save SongHub to your phone

Install a shortcut to your Home Screen and prepare songs for offline search.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen (or Open as Web App, if offered).
  4. Tap Add, then open SongHub from your Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm, then launch SongHub from your Home Screen.
Using SongHub offline: While online, open the installed app and wait for the song list to load. Test by switching off Wi-Fi and mobile data, then reopening SongHub. Offline search works only after the app files and song list have been saved by your browser. Clearing website data may remove them.
